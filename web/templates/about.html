Dissertation Project Overview
PhD Research Platform

This project investigates the empirical efficacy of Deep Reinforcement Learning algorithms operating in stochastic, partially observable, multi-agent competitive board games, specifically 4-player competitive Ludo.

Ludo exhibits unique challenges including: (1) highly stochastic transition probabilities driven by discrete 6-sided dice rolls, (2) severe action masking constraints where only a subset of tokens can legally advance, (3) non-linear strategic trade-offs between racing tokens forward and capturing opponent pieces to send them back to the yard.

State Encoding
176 Dimensions

A vectorized spatial representation encoding token positions across standard tracks, safe squares, home paths, relative distance matrices, and normalized dice rolls.

Comparative Baseline
4 DRL Models

Evaluates value-based (Double Dueling DQN + PER), on-policy policy gradients (Actor-Critic PPO), maximum entropy actor-critic (Discrete SAC), and distributional RL (Categorical C51).

Statistical Rigor
Zero Fabrication

Evaluation over 200,000 matches with balanced seating, independent seed sets, parametric/non-parametric tests, effect sizes, and strict distinction between pilot and final data.
